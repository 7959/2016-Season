When a Dc Motor is declared:

m[number]"[Motor Name]"

When a Servo is declared:

s[number]"[Servo Name]"

When a Dc Motor's speed is changed:

ms[Motor Number];[Speed]

When a Servo's position is changed:

sp[Servo Number];[New Position]

When a Servo's position is changed relatively:

sr[Servo Number];[Change in Position]
